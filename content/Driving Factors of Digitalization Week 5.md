

### What did I do?

For this topic I had a look at the [Wikipedia entry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_transformation) on digital transformation.  
I thought it was quite interesting to see that the entry is quite short - maybe because the topic is too big?  
In any case I also found out that although it is an omnipresent topic, it's difficult to measure the general impact of digital transformation on a macro level.  
I think this is interesting because I remember reading about the missing impact of AI a while ago. Companies feel compelled to use AI even though they have not yet figured out how to use it to their benefit, and they end up integrating it poorly - increasing cost and not really improving efficiency proportionally. It is framed as an [execution gap](https://www.consultancy.eu/news/14088/european-companies-face-ai-execution-gap-as-79-of-initiatives-miss-their-goals), but I could also imagine that it is a type of digital transformation gone wrong.
