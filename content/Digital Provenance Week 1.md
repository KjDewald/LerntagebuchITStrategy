## Self reflection after class

I think the overarching issue for a lot of information systems is traceability. This is true in cyber security, as well as in software engineering (traceable requirements). 

There seem to be no cures, only short term ailments to current issues. That makes the field super dynamic. It will be interesting to see how it evolves.

Also considering the rise of AI - Data Provenance gets more important not just because AI can create untrustworthy data, but also because the data fed to the AI needs to be trustworthy. 
If the issue isn't solved satisfyingly, missing data provenance could prove to be the downfall of AI, since the true assets of most models is the comprehensive and qualitatively high training data they are based on.
